# Trust

## What the AI can and cannot do

The assistant can read your administration and prepare changes. It cannot post
anything to Exact Online on its own.

- **Every write is a draft.** Creating, updating, or deleting a record is
  always prepared first and shown to you for review. Nothing is posted to
  Exact Online until you explicitly confirm it.
- **Extra warnings on the risky stuff.** A large amount, or a delete action,
  gets an additional warning in the confirmation step, so it stands out before
  you approve it.
- **Write protection is configurable per organization.** An owner can set a
  write mode per role (owner, admin, member), for example read-only for
  members and full access for admins. Individual users can also get an
  exception that overrides their role's default. A daily cap limits how many
  write actions a single user can perform per day. All of this is set by your
  organization, not by us.
- **Everything is scoped to the administration you selected.** A query or
  action only touches the administration you have selected; it does not reach
  across your other administrations.
- **The connection runs under your own Exact Online permissions.** It uses the
  access rights you already have in Exact Online and cannot grant you
  anything beyond that.

## Plans

- **Essentials** covers day-to-day questions and processing, with approval on
  every write.
- **Analytics** adds large-dataset analysis and trend overviews on top of
  Essentials, for organizations that want to look across large volumes of
  data rather than individual records.
