# Exact Online AI Connect

Exact Online AI Connect connects your Exact Online administration to the AI assistant you already use, so you can ask questions, pull reports, and prepare bookings for approval, right from the chat.

Hosted at `https://mcp-exactonline.iwebdevelopment.com`. You authorize access in your browser (OAuth), there are no tokens to paste into any config.

- **Install:** see the [README on GitHub](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/exact-online-ai-connect) for the full setup steps.
- **Clients:** pick your assistant in the Clients section in the navigation for exact, per-client instructions and plan requirements. The README's client compatibility matrix has the full overview at a glance.
- **Trust & privacy:** see [TRUST.md](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/exact-online-ai-connect/blob/main/TRUST.md) and [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/exact-online-ai-connect/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) on GitHub.
