# Security and privacy

Exact Online AI Connect is built and hosted by iWebDevelopment, which is ISO 27001
certified. Core infrastructure runs in the EU.

- Every write action is prepared as a draft you approve before anything is posted.
- The connection runs under your own Exact Online permissions.
- The authoritative terms are in iWeb's
  [Algemene Voorwaarden (AV)](https://www.iwebdevelopment.com/hubfs/144641067/Files/algemene-voorwaarden.pdf) and the DPA.

- Privacy and data handling: https://github.com/iwebdevnl/exact-online-ai-connect/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
